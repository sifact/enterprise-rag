## New vendor FAQ

Answers to common questions about bringing on a new supplier.

### How long does the security review take?

The vendor security review usually takes ten business days from the moment the vendor returns the completed security questionnaire. Reviews for vendors that will process customer data can take up to twenty business days.

### Who signs the contract?

Contracts are signed by the budget owner once legal and procurement have approved them.

### How are vendors paid?

Vendors are paid by bank transfer on net-30 terms after the invoice is approved in the procurement system.
