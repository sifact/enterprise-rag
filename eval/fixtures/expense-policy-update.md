## Travel and expense policy update

This page summarizes the changes to the travel and expense policy that take effect at the start of the third quarter.

### Lodging

The nightly hotel limit for business travel is now 250 US dollars in most cities and 350 US dollars in high-cost cities such as New York, London, Tokyo and San Francisco. Book through the company travel tool so the booking shows up in your expense report automatically.

### Meals

Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to 75 US dollars per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved team event.

### Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports within 30 days of the trip. Reports older than 90 days need approval from your manager and a finance business partner.
